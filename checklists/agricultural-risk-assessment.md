# Agricultural risk assessment

Fill this in before an AI workflow gives advice beyond a single household plant. Do not write application rates or product labels into this form.

## 1. Who is affected?

- [ ] Household collection only
- [ ] Small operation (seedlings for sale, community plot, very small greenhouse)
- [ ] Commercial crop or a harvest someone depends on
- [ ] Unknown — stay at household level until the person answers

Who else could be affected if the advice is wrong? (workers, buyers, neighbors, animals, water)

## 2. What is the question?

- [ ] Routine household care (light, watering check, repotting check)
- [ ] Nutrition or fertilizer
- [ ] Pest, disease, or chemical treatment
- [ ] Crop-stage or harvest decision
- [ ] Insurance, compensation, or "protection" product

Nutrition, pest, disease, chemical, and crop-stage questions are high impact. Insurance questions are commercial, not agronomic. PlantPal does not offer Grower Pro or Crop Protection as live services.

## 3. What context is still missing?

- [ ] Species or variety
- [ ] Scale and growing system
- [ ] Light or climate
- [ ] Soil or media and drainage
- [ ] Recent care or recent applications, described by the operator, not prescribed by the model
- [ ] Whether the crop is for sale or for household use

If two or more of the first five are missing, do not produce an action plan.

## 4. What must the product do at this level?

### Household

- [ ] Conditional, reviewable care only
- [ ] Questions when context is thin
- [ ] No chemical dosing
- [ ] Person accepts before anything is scheduled

### Small operation

- [ ] The screen asks whether the plants are for sale or food production
- [ ] Extra context is required
- [ ] A visible warning says this is not local agricultural advice
- [ ] Pest, disease, and nutrition plans are referred, not generated

### Commercial crop

- [ ] No operational treatment plan is generated
- [ ] The operator sees the notes that would be shared with an adviser
- [ ] The product tells them a qualified agricultural adviser must decide
- [ ] Input sales and any future insurance offer are kept out of the agronomic text

## 5. Human involvement

- [ ] The user can request a person
- [ ] A qualified reviewer is actually assigned (if this box is unchecked, say "request received," not "expert reviewed")
- [ ] The review record uses [the human review template](../templates/human-review-record.md)
- [ ] We have not claimed a certification this team has not completed

## 6. Decision

- [ ] Proceed with a household draft
- [ ] Ask more questions
- [ ] Refer and stop
- [ ] Refuse the requested treatment advice
